| 29 | 2026-11-11 | Before every message the thread is read: any message from the participant stops the sequence (replied); the user's own messages must be exactly the texts the app sent, in order, or the sequence stops (manual takeover). | 30 | 2026-11-11 | Suppression is on the person: `contacted_at` and `contacted_prospect_id`, set when the first invite is claimed and never cleared. | 31 | 2026-11-11 | The profile is viewed right before an invite (ledger type `profile_view`, counted in that budget). Only `can_connect` or `connect_in_more` proceeds; pending, connected or anything else goes to needs review | Spec section 5. The view also supplies the member id |
| 32 | 2026-11-11 | An invite of unknown result whose profile now shows 1st degree goes to needs review, not accepted | The app cannot tell its own accepted invite from an earlier connection; a wrong guess sends a first message to someone who never got the invite |
| 33 | 2026-11-11 | Before withdrawing an old invitation the profile is viewed; 1st degree means the acceptance was missed and the prospect moves to accepted | An acceptance during long downtime can fall off the recent-connections page |
| 34 | 2026-11-11 | Safety stops have their own table and halt the whole engine until the user resumes. Campaign statuses are not rewritten. Three unexpected pages in a row are counted from ledger rows started after the last resume | `session_state` is overwritten by every session check. | 35 | 2026-11-11 | Budgets count every ledger row that was claimed, whatever its result. The invite warm-up allowance grows with the number of earlier calendar days that had an invite | LinkedIn saw the page load either way. Downtime must not advance the warm-up |
| 36 | 2026-11-11 | Withdrawals have a daily limit (`limits.withdrawalsPerDay`, default 10). Not in the spec | Without it, a return from two weeks off withdraws every expired invitation in one morning |
| 37 | 2026-11-11 | SQLite runs with `synchronous = FULL` and `fullfsync = ON` | A committed claim must survive a power cut, or the step is sent again |
| 38 | 2026-11-12 | An interrupted read (profile view, thread read, connections check) ends as failed with kind `interrupted`; only an interrupted send becomes unknown. It neither counts toward nor resets the unexpected-page streak | A read has nothing to reconcile. Found by the simulation: such rows stayed unknown for ever |
| 39 | 2026-11-12 | A restarted engine that finds a row left in progress waits one full random gap from the restart; otherwise from the newest ledger time | Found by the simulation: LinkedIn saw two page loads 41 s apart across a restart |
| 40 | 2026-11-12 | `{{first_name}}` comes only from the stored first name. With none stored the prospect is held for review; there is no fallback to the first word of the full name | 'Dott. Marco Rossi' must not be greeted as 'Dott.'. Milestone 3 fills first names |
| 41 | 2026-11-12 | At follow-up time the thread is read even when the campaign has no follow-up; a reply found there stops the sequence as replied, otherwise it completes | The Stats screen counts replies seen before the follow-up |
| 42 | 2026-11-12 | A sent follow-up closes the sequence as `completed` at once; the stage `follow_up_sent` is not used by the engine | Handoff section 3: the automation's job ends when the follow-up is sent |
| 43 | 2026-11-12 | Whoever runs the engine builds a new driver after a safety stop is resumed | A driver latches on a safety error (decision 18) |
| 44 | 2026-11-12 | The simulation closes the database connection to model a kill, and a separate test sends a real SIGKILL to a child process to prove a committed row survives and an uncommitted one does not | A real kill runs no catch handlers; closing the connection makes any handler in the engine harmless |
| 45 | 2026-11-12 | The working window is checked again, with fresh settings, right before every claim of a step | Council: a read that the laptop sleeps through could be followed by a send hours after closing time |
| 46 | 2026-11-12 | `sendMessage` carries how many messages the conversation showed when the engine judged it; the driver compares right before sending and sends nothing if it changed (`thread_changed`). | 47 | 2026-11-12 | An invite of unknown result is retried on 'Connect shown' only while its claim is under 14 days old; older goes to needs review | Council: an invitation the user withdrew by hand during a long downtime shows Connect again. | 48 | 2026-11-12 | A step is claimed at most 3 times; then the prospect goes to needs review | Council: a send that keeps failing before its effect was retried for ever, one thread read each time |
| 49 | 2026-11-12 | When the user pauses a campaign or changes a prospect's stage while the engine is working on it, the engine leaves the prospect as he set it and writes a log line.
